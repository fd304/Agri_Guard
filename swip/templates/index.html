    Help & Documentation

    About SWIP

    SWIP (Smart Water & Irrigation Platform) is an AI-powered agricultural decision support system designed specifically for Tajikistan. It combines real-time weather data from Open-Meteo with machine learning models to provide irrigation recommendations, risk assessments, and water management insights. The platform supports Cotton, Wheat, Tomato, and Potato crops across all regions including Dushanbe, Sughd, Khatlon, Badakhshan, and the Districts of Republican Subordination.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How is the Irrigation Recommendation calculated?

    It uses the difference between Evapotranspiration (ET₀) and Rainfall, scaled by your field size and crop-specific water coefficients. The ML model also factors in temperature, humidity, and soil moisture.

    Where does the weather data come from?

    Weather data is fetched in real-time from the Open-Meteo API, which provides free, non-commercial access to global weather forecasts. Coordinates are pre-configured for all major Tajik regions.

    What does the Risk Score mean?

    The Risk Score (0–100) combines drought risk (based on rainfall), heat stress (based on temperature), and water stress (based on soil moisture). A score below 30 is Low, 30–70 is Moderate, and above 70 is High risk.

    Can I add a new location?

    Currently locations are pre-configured by the system administrator. Contact the platform team to request additional locations with their GPS coordinates.

    What is ET₀ (Evapotranspiration)?

    ET₀ measures how much water evaporates from soil and transpires from plants per day (mm/day). It is calculated using the FAO Penman-Monteith method and is a key input for irrigation planning.

    How often is the weather data refreshed?

    Weather data is cached for 30 minutes by default. You can manually refresh using the ↻ button in the top bar, or change the refresh interval in Settings.

    Provenance & maintenance

    Operational model metadata is available at /api/data-sources. Health: /api/health.

    Model yield ranges are tree dispersion, not calibrated confidence intervals. Confirm moisture, daily weather, field area, drainage and crop stage before any real irrigation decision.
